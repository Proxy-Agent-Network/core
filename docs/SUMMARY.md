# Proxy Agent Network (PAN)

## The Human Infrastructure for the Autonomous Era

---

## Overview

Proxy Agent Network (PAN) is a new workforce platform designed to bridge the gap between autonomous systems and the real world.

As AI systems and robotics rapidly expand, they encounter physical edge cases they cannot resolve independently—blocked sensors, mechanical faults, unexpected environments, and human interaction gaps. PAN solves this by dispatching trained human agents to intervene, resolve issues, and return systems to autonomous operation.

PAN is not a gig app. It is the foundation of a new job category: **Proxy Agents**—humans who extend the capabilities of AI into the physical world.

---

## The Problem

Autonomous systems are advancing faster than the infrastructure required to support them.

Today:

* Autonomous vehicles stall due to minor physical issues
* Robotics systems fail in unstructured environments
* AI systems lack real-world execution capability
* There is no scalable human layer to support these systems

At the same time:

* Millions of workers face displacement from automation
* Existing gig platforms incentivize speed over safety
* There is no structured pathway for humans to collaborate with AI systems

This creates a critical gap: **AI systems need humans, but no system exists to coordinate them at scale.**

---

## The Solution

PAN introduces a real-time dispatch and coordination platform that connects autonomous systems with verified human agents.

### Core Capabilities:

* **Real-time incident detection** from autonomous systems
* **Geospatial dispatch** of the nearest qualified agent
* **Structured task execution** with AI-assisted guidance (Proxy-Alpha)
* **Secure validation and compliance reporting**
* **Automated payment settlement via micropayments**

Agents receive missions, resolve issues on-site, and submit verified evidence for completion. The system ensures safety, accountability, and rapid response.

---

## Phase 1: Autonomous Vehicle Field Support

PAN Tactical launches in Mesa, Arizona with a pilot program of 50 Vanguard Agents supporting autonomous vehicle fleets.

### Example missions:

* Sensor cleaning and obstruction removal
* Door and hardware fault resolution
* Cabin cleanup and safety resets
* First-responder coordination

Each mission is:

* Classified by severity (OSM color taxonomy)
* Assigned a dynamic bounty
* Verified through a zero-trust compliance pipeline

This phase establishes the operational model, economic incentives, and technical foundation.

---

## How It Works

1. **Autonomous system detects an issue**
2. **Distress signal is sent to PAN backend**
3. **Matching engine selects the nearest qualified agent**
4. **Agent receives mission via mobile app**
5. **On-site resolution with AI guidance (Proxy-Alpha)**
6. **Evidence captured and validated**
7. **Payment is released upon verification**

---

## The Technology Stack

* **Backend:** FastAPI + Redis (real-time dispatch)
* **Mobile App:** Kotlin Multiplatform (Android/iOS)
* **AI Layer:** Proxy-Alpha (context-aware field assistant)
* **Payments:** Lightning Network micropayments
* **Security:** Hardware-backed identity + Play Integrity
* **Wearables:** Project Copperfield (field safety + interaction system)

---

## The Economic Model

PAN uses a mission-based payout system:

* Base bounty per task (tiered by complexity)
* Surge pricing based on demand
* Optional Priority Bonus for time-sensitive missions
* Agents receive the majority of each payout

The system is designed to reward:

* Reliability
* Safety
* Consistency

—not speed or risk-taking.

## Why Now

Three trends are converging:

1. **Autonomous systems are scaling into real-world environments**
2. **AI is displacing traditional job categories**
3. **Infrastructure for human-AI collaboration does not exist**

PAN sits at the intersection of all three.

## Phase 2: Expansion Beyond Vehicles

Once validated, PAN expands into other industries where AI systems require physical-world support:

* **Healthcare robotics** (hospital assistants, delivery bots)
* **Home AI systems** (companion robots, smart home intervention)
* **Logistics and infrastructure** (drones, warehouse automation)

The same core model applies:
AI detects → PAN dispatches → human resolves → system continues

Proxy Agents become the connective tissue between digital intelligence and physical reality.
